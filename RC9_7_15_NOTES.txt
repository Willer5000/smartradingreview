RC9.7.15 — STRUCTURAL ENTRY LOCATION + LOWER-TF EXECUTION CONFIRMATION
Baseline: RC9.7.14 FINAL FINAL already deployed.
Repository: MAIN only.
SQL: none.
Research: none.
Frontend: none.

PURPOSE
- Separate "candidate reaction zone" from "executable Entry" more rigorously.
- Make Entry behavior symmetric by structural location:
  LONG at ceiling/extension -> wait for pullback/reaction zone.
  LONG at floor/demand -> a near-market Entry may be valid, but Futures still needs reaction confirmation when near market.
  SHORT at floor/extension -> wait for rebound/reaction zone.
  SHORT at ceiling/supply -> a near-market Entry may be valid, with the same confirmation logic.

WHAT CHANGES
1) app.py
- Entry selection now classifies structural location using real supports/resistances first.
- ATR is a distance normalizer/fallback, not the source of the Entry.
- Order Blocks, FVG, support/resistance and POC remain primary reaction zones.
- Fibonacci remains a secondary candidate/confluence.
- EMA9/21/50/200 can add confluence only when already near a valid structural POI; EMA does not create a trade by itself.
- Near-market Entries are penalized when chasing a LONG at ceiling or SHORT at floor.
- Near-market structural Entries are allowed when LONG is at floor/demand or SHORT is at ceiling/supply.
- The artificial minimum pullback is relaxed only on the correct structural side; leveraged Futures still has the lower-TF trigger gate.
- New diagnostics are persisted in levels: entry_market_location, entry_location_context, entry_location_basis, range position, directional extension, EMA confluence, and ATR role.

2) entry_reaction_engine.py
- Near-market Futures 1H, 2H and 4H Entries require a lower-TF closed reaction.
- 12H and 1D keep their stricter lower-TF confirmation requirement.
- A deeper structural limit remains eligible without requiring a reaction before price reaches it.

3) futures_system.py
- Lower-TF mapping: 1H->30M, 2H->30M, 4H->1H, 12H->2H, 1D->4H.
- Timing gate now uses structural location, not ATR extension alone.
- LONG at ceiling/extension near market => WAIT_PULLBACK_LONG_EXTENDED.
- SHORT at floor/extension near market => WAIT_REBOUND_SHORT_EXTENDED.
- Current public order-flow/microstructure remains SHADOW_CONFLUENCE_NOT_AUTHORITY. It is intentionally not promoted to a production veto until OOS calibration proves it.

NOT CHANGED
- Direction/thesis engine.
- Strategy Bank / ReviewTrader learning.
- Guardian.
- Leverage.
- Signal validity/lifecycle.
- Telegram.
- Supabase schema.
- Render Free hardening.

TRADING RATIONALE
A good directional thesis is not enough for a good Entry. Entry is now evaluated as:
structural location -> reaction zone -> confluence -> timing confirmation -> executable Entry.
This avoids using ATR as an empirical price generator while retaining ATR for normalization of distance/volatility.

IMPORTANT
No Entry can be guaranteed to win. RC9.7.15 raises execution quality requirements; it does not convert technical quality scores into TP probabilities.
