RC9.7.16 — USER-FACING SIGNAL CARD

Scope: presentation only.

Changed:
- Saved Futures signal detail no longer exposes internal strategy IDs such as
  FUT_LONG_BREAKOUT_RETEST__FUTURES__MEDIUM__4H__LONG.
- Internal strategy families are translated to trader-friendly names.
- THESIS+DEFAULT / internal source is not shown to users.
- Internal specialist/trader names are stripped from visible evidence.
- MTF/Safety/Entry quality/reachability/defensibility labels are translated to
  clear Spanish for a basic trader.
- Real indicator evidence is shown when available: ADX, DMI, RSI, MACD,
  volume ratio and ATR volatility.
- Internal ReviewTrader post-trade states remain unchanged in backend but are
  translated in the UI.
- No score is described as a guaranteed TP probability.

Not changed:
- Strategy selection.
- ReviewTrader storage/learning.
- Entry/SL/TP.
- Safety.
- Leverage.
- Guardian.
- Signal validity/lifecycle.
- Telegram.
- Supabase schema.
- Research.
